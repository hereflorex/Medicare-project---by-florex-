ABOUT THE PROJECT

MediCare — Hospital Management System

A full-stack college project created to demonstrate patient and doctor management, authentication, appointments, prescriptions, profiles and billing through a modern web interface.

Project purpose

MediCare is a BCA college project focused on building a practical hospital-management workflow. The system connects patients and doctors through role-based accounts and database-backed features.

Technology

Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL/SQLite, HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. The interface is responsive and includes theme support, animations, loading states and toast feedback.

Developed by

Tirth & Dhvanit
M.L.J. Gandhi Institute of Technology
BCA · 2026

Core modules

Patient registration and login, doctor registration and login, profiles, doctor discovery, appointments, prescriptions, billing, availability and role-aware dashboards.
